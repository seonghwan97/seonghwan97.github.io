\section{Experience}

\role{Camera-Only Worker Safety Monitoring}{Research Assistant, iConSenSe Lab, Illinois Institute of Technology}{Jan 2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a camera-only worker monitoring system on ordinary site video, no wearables. A YOLO
        pose model extracts keypoints, ByteTrack holds worker identity across frames, and downstream
        heads roll activity, work state, and posture into a 10-second exposure score at a median
        23 fps for 3 to 9 workers on an RTX 2060.
  \item Checked every module against an independent reference across 42 subjects, reaching 0.861
        activity accuracy, 0.775 work-state F1, and 0.793 correlation with a 3D-pose ergonomic
        reference. Scoped workload as an exposure indicator rather than a fatigue measurement after
        it reached only 0.526 against indirect calorimetry.
  \item Built the CO\textsubscript{2} sensing rig on Raspberry Pi for the field cross-check, and
        separately lifted 2D poses to 3D with MotionBERT across 61 HoloLens 2 sessions. Presented at
        the BuiltWorlds Global Summit and received a \$12,500 research grant.
\end{itemize}

\role{Fine-Grained Road Surface Classifier}{Research Assistant, MMCom Lab, Illinois Institute of Technology}{Jan 2025 -- Jul 2026}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Trained a classifier over 27 road-surface types on RSCD, roughly 960k training images
        against a 50k test split. Traced most errors to a long-tailed class distribution and to
        surface types that look nearly identical in a single frame.
  \item Fixed the data before the model, filling out rare classes with diffusion-generated images,
        then tried multi-task and mixture-of-experts heads to separate the look-alike classes.
  \item Pushed EfficientNet-B0 from 91.70\% to 93.33\% over a standard-augmentation baseline.
\end{itemize}

\role{Image-Based Process Control for Microfluidic Mixing}{Research Assistant, Bhushan Research Group, Illinois Institute of Technology}{Jan 2025 -- May 2026}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Wrote the measurement code that registers and normalizes CFD contour and experimental
        fluorescence images, then computes a 0 to 1 mixing index from outlet pixel intensity.
  \item Benchmarked SVR, MLP, XGBoost, Random Forest, and linear regression as CFD surrogates. SVR
        won on held-out data with MAE 0.0014 and R\textsuperscript{2} 0.9998, close enough to stand
        in for a full CFD solve during inverse screening for inlet conditions that hit a target
        mixing value, which I then confirmed with fresh CFD runs and six fluorescence experiments.
\end{itemize}

\role{Two-Stage Parcel Inventory Recognition}{Undergraduate Researcher, VCL Lab, Seoul National University of Science and Technology}{Jan 2023 -- Dec 2023}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built two-stage recognition for photos of stacked parcels. DINO and YOLO detect product
        regions and Weighted Boxes Fusion merges them into cleaner boxes than either alone, then
        EfficientNet-B7 with test-time augmentation classifies each crop.
  \item Placed 1st of 19 teams, identifying 56.6\% of the 53 evaluation products against 49.1\% for
        the runner-up, at 0.80 mean IoU, the only team to pair the top recognition rate with
        localization above 0.75 IoU.
\end{itemize}
